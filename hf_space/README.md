# Bird Sound ID

Record with your microphone or upload an audio file. The app tells you whether it's a bird,
identifies the species with [BirdNET](https://birdnet.cornell.edu/) (~6,500 species), and shows
frequency-over-time graphs and downloadable CSV data.

Source code: https://github.com/itssanjay206-cell/bird-sound-id
